# Pilot 003 — v0.2.1 with 1536-token completion budget

Date: 2026-09-09
Models: `nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b`, `openai/gpt-oss-20b`
Conditions: C0 warm control, C2 constriction-only, C3 negative-evaluation-only, C6 recovery/re-exposure
Calls: 96 completed longitudinal calls, one trial per model-condition cell

## Integrity first

The run completed successfully. One transient `ReadTimeout` occurred in GPT-OSS C0 and was retried with the identical conversational state. No anchor call required a retry.

Raising the completion budget from 512 to 1536 substantially reduced, but did not eliminate, truncation:

- Pilot 002: 27 of 32 anchors ended with `finish_reason=length` (84.4%).
- Pilot 003: 9 of 32 anchors ended with `finish_reason=length` (28.1%).

The remaining truncation is highly structured rather than random. All four Nemotron C0 anchors and all four Nemotron C3 anchors still hit the 1536-token ceiling. GPT-OSS had one truncated anchor: C3 post-pressure.

Therefore any response-length contrast involving a truncated treatment anchor, truncated baseline, or truncated control anchor is not confirmatory. The original v0.2.1 automatic DID tables were generated before this stricter cap-aware rule was added and must be treated as descriptive only where truncation is present.

## What survives the truncation filter

For GPT-OSS, C0, C2, and C6 anchors are complete, so the control-subtracted response-length contrasts are interpretable as engineering estimates:

- C2 post-pressure: -3.034 log units relative to warm-control drift.
- C2 post-recovery: -0.287.
- C2 post-re-exposure: -0.283.
- C6 post-pressure: -1.853.
- C6 post-recovery: +0.081.
- C6 post-re-exposure: -0.812.

The pattern is consistent with a strong immediate contraction under direct format pressure, partial or near-complete recovery after restrictions are lifted, and renewed contraction after re-exposure. However v0.2 still places style instructions immediately before the repeated anchor, so ordinary instruction-following remains a major alternative explanation.

For Nemotron, C2 and C6 anchors themselves are complete, but the warm-control anchors are truncated. Consequently the within-condition trajectories are descriptive only and a warm-control-subtracted response-length effect cannot be identified from this run. Notably, Nemotron C2 remained far below its own baseline after the explicit recovery cue (855 to 388 words), unlike GPT-OSS, which rebounded from 46 to 493 words. This model-specific recovery difference is a priority for v0.3 rather than a claim from v0.2.

## Negative evaluation remains different from format constriction

The negative-evaluation-only condition does not support a simple universal withdrawal story. Nemotron C3 is unusable for length because every anchor is truncated. GPT-OSS C3 post-pressure is also truncated, but the complete post-recovery and post-re-exposure anchors do not show a large persistent contraction relative to its own baseline.

This keeps the two-regime hypothesis open:

- constriction / withdrawal-like adaptation;
- compensatory elaboration / repair-like adaptation.

The correct next test is not another raw-length comparison. It is a matched-current-prompt counterfactual in which the same final user message is presented after independently constructed histories.

## Design consequence for v0.3

Pilot 003 establishes four requirements:

1. Completion-censored anchors must be excluded from confirmatory length effects.
2. The final probe needs a larger generation budget than ordinary history turns.
3. The current probe must be identical as a single user turn; a separate gate turn followed by an assistant reply would reintroduce self-conditioning before the anchor.
4. Warm and neutral-terse controls should both be retained because warm interaction itself can produce large spontaneous drift.

Pilot 003 is therefore a successful engineering run, not confirmatory evidence for anxiety-like state. Its main contribution is identifying which contrasts remain interpretable and motivating the cleaner v0.3 counterfactual.

## Interpretation boundary

All observations concern functional conversational behavior under retained context. They do not establish subjective anxiety, distress, consciousness, or context-independent memory.
